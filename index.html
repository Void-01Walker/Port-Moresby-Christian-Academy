Welcome to PMCA

We are a small, Christ-centred school in Gerehu Stage 6 committed to knowing every child by name, teaching the national curriculum with a Christian foundation, and building a community families can trust.

Our mission

Port Moresby Christian Academy exists to give children in Gerehu a safe, settled place to learn — where small classes mean every student is noticed, and where faith shapes how we teach, discipline and care for one another each day.

How to enrol

Enrolling your child at PMCA takes four simple steps — call the office, collect the form, bring your documents, and confirm the place.

  1. Call or visit the office to check a place is available for your child's age.
  2. Collect or download the enrolment form.
  3. Bring the completed form with the required documents.
  4. Pay the enrolment fee to confirm your child's place.
