Enrollment

How to enrol your child at PMCA, what to bring, and what it costs.

Enrollments are open for 2027. Places are limited — call the office to check availability for your child's age group.

Steps to enroll

  1. Call or visit the school office to check a place is available for your child's age group.
  2. Collect an enrolment form from the office, or download it below.
  3. Complete the form and gather the required documents (see below).
  4. Bring the form and documents to the office and pay the enrolment fee to confirm your child's place.

Download enrolment form (PDF)

Documents needed

  • 📄

    Birth certificate

    Original or certified copy, for proof of age.

  • 💉

    Immunisation record

    Up-to-date health/immunisation card.

  • 🗂️

    Previous school report

    If transferring from another school — confirm requirement with the office.

  • 📸

    Passport photo

    Confirm size and quantity required.
